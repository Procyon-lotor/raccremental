Woah, you really made it all the way to the end! Congratulations!

If you are reading this right now, that means you have played through all the content I've made so far, and for that I genuinely want to express my sincere and immense thanks. The idea of someone taking the time to try out my game, let alone enjoying it enough to play through all the way to the end, just makes me so happy and gives me all the motivation I need to keep making games like this, regardless of how crazy they drive me. Whether you thought this was your favorite incremental game ever, or just needed something to kill a few hours, thank you for spending that time with me.

I do apologize if this was kind of an anticlimactic end to the game, or if you expected something big to happen after Awakening. Balancing started to become very delicate as more and more formulas were introduced, and also school is almost certainly going to get a lot more busy as midterms approach right about now. I don't know if I'll add more content to the game; I'm leaning towards no, just because this code file is already over 12,000 lines long and in many places unoptimal, and as I had mentioned before, I became a little obsessive and crazy with this project as I neared the end of it and I don't want it to affect my life like that anymore. If I do add more content, it will be in the form of a "Chapter 2", where new content is pretty much separate from what you've just played, with newer currencies and features having little impact on previous ones, but I wouldn't get your hopes up for it.

Purr purr purrrrr...sorry where was I? Oh yeah, seriously, thank you for playing the game, I really do want you to know I appreciate you. If you find any bugs, want to suggest something (preferably something small), or just want to say hi, feel free to join my Discord by clicking the button in the bottom-left corner!

I wish I had more to say given how much this game means to me, but to avoid sounding cloying I'll just leave it here. For now though, enjoy the rest of your day or night, and may the Racc King guide your spirit as you carry on.

Much love,

Procyon

P.S. Don't forget to turn off the apple teleporters before you leave! We're having raccoon interns at the factory this week and we are NOT failing another safety inspection! :3
